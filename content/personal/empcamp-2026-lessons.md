I was fortunate enough to go to [EMFCamp 2026](./empcamp-2026.md) this year.

And I wanted to share my lessons from this year for myself to remember the next time I go, and for the people going in future years.

## Arriving and Leaving

One of the first mistakes I made was to arrive on the day it is scheduled to open and the day it is scheduled to close.

And while arriving on the opening day is the correct thing to do (unless you're helping with setup of course), then I made a mistake of leaving at midday on the advertised closing day, the Sunday.

Which probably did have the advantage of leaving before the rush, it did mean I missed the closing ceremony and a handful of talks.

While it does say it finishes on the 19th (Sunday) the festival does remain open until Monday to give people a chance make the most of the Sunday.
